Google releases Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, built for long agentic jobs, deep science and cyber defense

New York opens its AI safety registration portal, putting OpenAI and Anthropic under new oversight

TechCrunch reported Tuesday, November 10, 2026, that New York opened a registration portal that large frontier AI developers like Anthropic and OpenAI must use to register by January 1, 2027, when the RAISE Act kicks in. The law requires large frontier developers, those with more than $500 million in annual revenue, to publish safety frameworks, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours and issue public model transparency reports. A newly formed Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity, and Trust (DIGIT) will oversee the labs, with penalties up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million for subsequent ones. Gov. Kathy Hochul also launched the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research at SUNY Binghamton to independently evaluate advanced AI systems.

MiniMax releases H3-Image, an open-weight model that generates and edits native 4K images

Crypto Briefing reported Tuesday, November 10, 2026, that MiniMax released H3-Image on Hugging Face, through MiniMax Code and via its APIs. The 33.1-billion-parameter diffusion transformer handles both text-to-image generation and editing, producing native images of about 16 megapixels, including 4096×4096 and widescreen sizes up to 6272×2688. It needs about six sampling steps, accepts up to 16 reference images, and costs roughly $0.022 per 1K image and $0.069 per 4K image through the API. The catch: its community license excludes use in the US, the EU, the UK and South Korea, and larger companies need written authorization.

Nvidia posts open weights for Alpamayo 2 Edge, a 2-billion-parameter driving model that explains its moves

IA Compa reported Tuesday, November 10, 2026, that Nvidia published the weights for Alpamayo 2 Edge on Hugging Face. It is a 2-billion-parameter vision-language-action model for autonomous driving that pairs a Cosmos 3 Edge reasoning backbone with a diffusion action expert to produce driving trajectories and explanations of them. It is optimized for Nvidia DRIVE AGX Thor and designed for 10 Hz in-vehicle operation, and the release includes deployment recipes and evaluation tools. The weights are under the OpenMDW-1.1 license and the code under Apache 2.0; Nvidia also released AlpaJudge, a human-calibrated evaluation and reward model.

A former Marine who isn’t a professional developer built Battle Buddy, an AI agent that helps veterans find their benefits

Microsoft’s Source reported November 9, 2026, that Gary Crull, who served in the Marine Corps and the Army National Guard and now works as an analyst at the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, built Battle Buddy, an AI agent that helps veterans, families and caregivers make sense of federal, state and local benefits. He built it with low-code tools, training it on VA regulations and about 1,000 questions and answers drawn from veteran forums so it learns both what veterans ask and how they ask it. Some testers say they saved hours; others found information they had sought for more than a year. It is in open field testing, including at the VA Augusta Medical Center in Georgia. “Battle Buddy is made by veterans for veterans,” Crull says.

HKUST builds an AI “ASD Super Agent” to help families spot autism early and turn big goals into small daily steps

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology announced on November 11, 2026, that a team led by Prof. Kani Chen developed ASD Super Agent, an AI platform for autism support that combines early screening, personalized intervention and continuous family follow-up. Parents can record short videos of a child’s daily activities and complete questionnaires to get a risk report to bring to professionals; in preliminary testing it scored an AUC of 0.92, and HKUST says more clinical validation will come before deployment. Parents can describe challenges like emotional regulation or communication and receive tailored suggestions drawn from more than 20 recognized intervention approaches. The team spoke with nearly 500 parents while building it, and the platform helps break long-term goals into manageable daily actions.
